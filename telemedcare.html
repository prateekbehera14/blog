Why TeleMedCare?

India has 70% of its population living in rural areas, but 80% of doctors are based in cities. Patients in villages travel 50–100 km and spend ₹500–₹2,000 just to reach a specialist — often delaying treatment until conditions worsen.

TeleMedCare lets a farmer in a remote village consult the same specialist they would see in the city, from any basic smartphone, in just a few clicks.
