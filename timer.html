Your time, at your pace.

Workout repeats your work and rest intervals, including the final rest. Timer counts down once. Stopwatch counts up. Switching modes pauses the outgoing session; Reset unlocks its settings.

Every work interval ends with a 5-second alarm before rest starts. These alarm intervals are included in the plan's total time, even when muted or rest is set to 0. A short tone marks the next work interval. A 5-second alarm also marks timer completion and the end of the final rest; without rest, the last work alarm finishes the session without a duplicate alarm. Sound needs a click to activate. Pause, Mute, Reset, starting again, or switching modes stops the sound immediately.

Make it yours. Choose Sky, Mint, Sunset, or the dark Midnight theme from the top bar. Themes apply to every mode without interrupting your session. Your choice is saved in this browser when local storage is available.
