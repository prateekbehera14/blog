Your time, at your pace.

Workout runs an optional get-ready countdown, then repeats your work and rest intervals. Rest after the final round is optional. Timer counts down once. Stopwatch counts up. Switching modes pauses the outgoing session; Reset unlocks its settings.

Alerts overlap your intervals and never add time. Open Settings (the sliders button, or the summary chips under Start) to edit the workout plan or timer length and the alerts. Under Alerts & sound, choose the sound and volume, whether a cue plays when a round starts, when rest starts and when time is up, how many countdown beeps play before each interval ends, and how long the finish alarm rings. Sound needs a click to activate. Pause, Mute, Reset, starting again, or switching modes stops the sound immediately.

Make it yours. Choose Sky, Mint, Sunset, or the dark Midnight theme from the top bar. Themes apply to every mode without interrupting your session. Your choice is saved in this browser when local storage is available.

Keep this page open and your device awake. Background tabs can delay alarms. Sleep or browser suspension can prevent on-time sound; elapsed progress is reconciled when execution resumes. Screen wake lock is attempted where supported, but is not guaranteed.
